# ScrimBoard — free points tables for PUBG Mobile & Free Fire scrims

**100% free · works in any browser · no account · no install · Arabic & English**

### ▶️ [Open ScrimBoard](https://q8core.com/scrim/)

<img width="720" height="900" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1a3e500c-4b01-428f-8948-a206dba97589" />


Run a PUBG Mobile or Free Fire scrim or tournament and get the standings in seconds.

- **Points tables:** enter each match, and the overall standings update automatically. Default points: PUBG Mobile PMGC (10-6-5-4-3-2-1-1, +1 per kill) and Free Fire FFWS (12-9-8-7-6-5-4-3-2-1, +1 per kill). You can edit both.
- **Fair tie-breaks:** total points, then wins (WWCD / Booyah), placement points, kills, and the latest match.
- **Ready-to-post images:** 1080×1350 or 1080×1920, in Arabic or English, with your logo and colour, for WhatsApp, Discord and Instagram. No watermark.
- **Read result screenshots:** upload Free Fire results screenshots and the teams, places and kills are filled in for you. You check them before saving, and teams are recognised automatically after the first time. PUBG Mobile screenshot reading is being added.
- **Top fraggers:** player kills across all matches.
- **Backup and restore** your tournament as a file. Everything stays in your own browser.
